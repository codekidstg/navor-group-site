Who we are

A deliberately small structure.

NAVOR GROUP was founded by three complementary profiles: two IT engineers specialised in software architecture and project management, and a human resources director. We are not a large consultancy, and that is not a problem to be fixed — it is a choice. Every engagement passes directly through the hands of the people who scoped it. No layers of intermediaries, no diluted responsibility: you speak to the people who decide and who deliver, from the first conversation to closing.

Just as important

What we do not do.

We do not do graphic design or brand identity. We do not do organisational consulting. When a need falls outside what we do well, we say so rather than stretch to fit — it costs us a contract and saves you a bad one.

The same applies to scale. We are a small structure and we say so: we bid for work we can actually carry out with the resources we have. If an engagement exceeds our capacity, we say so before bidding rather than discover it mid-delivery.
